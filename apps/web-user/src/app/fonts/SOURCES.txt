Manrope (Latin subset) and Noto Sans Malayalam (Malayalam subset).
Original WOFF2 files returned by Google Fonts for the existing next/font/google configuration, preserved locally to remove build-time network requests.

Upstream sources:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansmalayalam
